Breathe

Sync your breath with the circle. Inhale as it grows, exhale as it shrinks.

Basic

Deep Belly Breathing

Place one hand on your stomach. Inhale deeply through your nose, feeling your stomach push out. Exhale slowly through your mouth. Repeat for 2 minutes.

Calming

Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril)

Close your right nostril with your thumb and inhale through the left. Close the left nostril, open the right, and exhale. Reverses stress and balances the mind.
