Explore Asanas

A complete library of 50 step-by-step postures for strength, flexibility, and mindfulness.

Standing

1. Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand tall with your feet together and arms at your sides.
  2. Distribute weight evenly across both feet.
  3. Inhale, roll shoulders back, and lift your chest.
  4. Tuck your tailbone slightly and look straight ahead.

Benefits: Improves posture and increases body awareness.

Balance

2. Vrikshasana (Tree Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Shift weight to your left leg.
  2. Place the sole of your right foot on your left inner thigh.
  3. Focus eyes on a single point to keep balance.
  4. Bring hands together at chest. Hold 5 breaths.

Benefits: Improves physical balance and strengthens legs.

Inversion

3. Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Start on hands and knees.
  2. Tuck toes under and lift hips toward the ceiling.
  3. Press chest toward your thighs.
  4. Keep knees slightly bent if needed. Hold 5 breaths.

Benefits: Stretches hamstrings and strengthens shoulders.

Backbend

4. Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie flat on your stomach.
  2. Place hands flat under your shoulders.
  3. Inhale and lift your head and chest off the floor.
  4. Keep elbows tucked in. Hold 3 breaths.

Benefits: Strengthens the spine and opens the chest.

Strength

5. Virabhadrasana I (Warrior I)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Step right foot forward, left foot back.
  2. Bend right knee over the ankle.
  3. Raise both arms straight up to the ceiling.
  4. Hold for 5 breaths, then switch legs.

Benefits: Builds stamina and stretches the chest.

Strength

6. Virabhadrasana II (Warrior II)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Step feet wide apart.
  2. Turn right foot out 90 degrees, bend the knee.
  3. Stretch arms out parallel to the floor.
  4. Gaze over your right hand. Hold 5 breaths.

Benefits: Opens the hips and strengthens the core.

Resting

7. Balasana (Child’s Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Kneel on the floor, sit on your heels.
  2. Bend forward and rest your forehead on the mat.
  3. Stretch arms forward or rest them by your sides.
  4. Breathe deeply for 1 minute.

Benefits: Relieves stress, fatigue, and lower back pain.

Flexibility

8. Paschimottanasana (Seated Forward Bend)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit with both legs extended straight.
  2. Inhale and sit up tall.
  3. Exhale and hinge at hips, reaching for toes.
  4. Hold for 5 to 10 breaths.

Benefits: Calms the brain and stretches hamstrings.

Backbend

9. Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat.
  2. Press into feet to lift hips toward the ceiling.
  3. Keep thighs parallel and squeeze glutes.
  4. Hold 5 breaths, then slowly roll spine down.

Benefits: Stretches the chest, neck, and spine.

Standing

10. Trikonasana (Triangle Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand with feet wide, turn right foot out.
  2. Raise arms parallel to floor.
  3. Reach right hand forward and lower it to your shin.
  4. Stretch left arm straight up. Look up.

Benefits: Relieves backache and stretches hips.

Core

11. Phalakasana (Plank Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Start in a push-up position.
  2. Keep shoulders directly over your wrists.
  3. Engage your core to keep your body in a straight line.
  4. Hold for 30 seconds while breathing steadily.

Benefits: Builds intense core and shoulder strength.

Backbend

12. Ustrasana (Camel Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Kneel on the mat with knees hip-width apart.
  2. Place hands on your lower back for support.
  3. Inhale, lean back slowly, and push hips forward.
  4. If comfortable, reach back to hold your heels.

Benefits: Stretches the entire front of the body.

Core

13. Navasana (Boat Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit on the floor with knees bent.
  2. Lean back slightly and lift your feet off the floor.
  3. Straighten your legs to form a 'V' shape.
  4. Extend arms parallel to the floor. Hold 5 breaths.

Benefits: Strengthens the abdomen, hip flexors, and spine.

Standing

14. Utkatasana (Chair Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand in Mountain Pose.
  2. Inhale and raise arms above your head.
  3. Exhale and bend knees, as if sitting in a chair.
  4. Keep chest lifted. Hold for 5-8 breaths.

Benefits: Tones the leg muscles and stimulates the heart.

Flexibility

15. Kapotasana (Pigeon Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. From downward dog, bring right knee behind right wrist.
  2. Extend left leg straight back on the mat.
  3. Keep hips square to the front.
  4. Slowly fold forward over your right leg.

Benefits: Extremely deep stretch for the hips and glutes.

Resting

16. Savasana (Corpse Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie completely flat on your back.
  2. Let your feet fall open to the sides naturally.
  3. Rest arms by your sides, palms facing up.
  4. Close your eyes and breathe naturally for 5 mins.

Benefits: Relaxes the whole body and reduces fatigue.

Backbend

17. Urdhva Mukha Svanasana (Upward Dog)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie on stomach, hands near your lower ribs.
  2. Press firmly into hands and tops of your feet.
  3. Lift your thighs and torso off the floor.
  4. Keep shoulders away from ears.

Benefits: Improves posture and stretches the abdomen.

Twist

18. Ardha Matsyendrasana

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit on the floor, cross right foot over left knee.
  2. Keep left leg straight or bent under you.
  3. Twist torso to the right, hooking left elbow on right knee.
  4. Look over your right shoulder. Hold 5 breaths.

Benefits: Energizes the spine and aids digestion.

Backbend

19. Dhanurasana (Bow Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie on your stomach.
  2. Bend knees and reach back to grab your ankles.
  3. Inhale and lift your chest and thighs off the floor.
  4. Look forward and hold for 3-5 breaths.

Benefits: Stretches the entire front body and ankles.

Balance

20. Ardha Chandrasana (Half Moon)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. From Triangle pose, bend right knee slightly.
  2. Place right fingertips on the floor in front of you.
  3. Lift left leg parallel to the floor.
  4. Extend left arm up. Hold for 5 breaths.

Benefits: Enhances coordination and leg strength.

Kids Favorite

21. Titli Asana (Butterfly Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit on the floor with a straight back.
  2. Bring the soles of your feet together.
  3. Hold feet firmly and gently flap knees up and down.
  4. Keep flapping like a butterfly for 30 seconds.

Benefits: Fun way to stretch the inner thighs.

Kids Favorite

22. Ananda Balasana (Happy Baby)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie flat on your back on a soft mat.
  2. Bring knees toward your chest.
  3. Grab the outside edges of your feet.
  4. Gently rock side to side like a happy baby!

Benefits: Highly relaxing and stretches lower back.

Kids Favorite

23. Marjaryasana (Cat Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Get on your hands and knees.
  2. Arch your back high towards the ceiling.
  3. Look at your belly button like a scared cat.
  4. Meow softly as you exhale.

Benefits: Makes the spine flexible and allows imagination.

Kids Favorite

24. Bitilasana (Cow Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. From Cat pose, drop your belly toward the floor.
  2. Lift your head and chest up to the sky.
  3. Smile and say "Moooo!" as you breathe out.
  4. Alternate between Cat and Cow 5 times.

Benefits: Warms up the spine and stretches the belly.

Kids Favorite

25. Simhasana (Lion Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit back on your heels.
  2. Spread fingers wide on your knees like claws.
  3. Inhale deeply through your nose.
  4. Exhale, stick tongue out, and roar "Haaa!"

Benefits: Relieves tension in the face and throat.

Kids Favorite

26. Dekasana (Airplane Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand tall with arms out like wings.
  2. Slowly lift one leg straight behind you.
  3. Lean chest forward to balance on one leg.
  4. Pretend to fly for 10 seconds, then switch.

Benefits: Improves focus, balance, and core strength.

Kids Favorite

27. Mandukasana (Frog Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Squat down with feet apart.
  2. Place hands flat on the floor between feet.
  3. Look straight ahead like a frog.
  4. Jump up in the air and land softly! Repeat 5 times.

Benefits: Burns off excess energy and builds leg strength.

Kids Favorite

28. Snake Pose (Cobra Variation)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Lie on your tummy with legs zipped together.
  2. Put hands under shoulders.
  3. Lift chest up and hiss like a snake "Ssss!"
  4. Slowly slither back down.

Benefits: Keeps the spine healthy and flexible.

Kids Favorite

29. Flamingo Pose (Tree Variation)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand on one leg like a pink flamingo.
  2. Bend the other knee up.
  3. Flap your arms gently like wings to keep balance.
  4. Hold for 5 seconds without falling over!

Benefits: Teaches patience and standing balance.

Kids Favorite

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Jump your feet wide apart.
  2. Stretch arms out wide to the sides.
  3. Spread fingers wide.
  4. Twinkle your fingers like a shining star!

Benefits: Opens the chest and brings joyful energy.

Kids Favorite

31. Crescent Moon Pose

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand tall with hands reaching up to the sky.
  2. Clasp your hands together.
  3. Lean your body sideways to look like a banana or moon.
  4. Hold, then lean to the other side.

Benefits: Stretches the sides of the tummy.

Kids Favorite

32. Kurmasana (Turtle Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit with legs in a wide diamond shape.
  2. Slide hands under your knees.
  3. Round your back like a turtle hiding in its shell.
  4. Rest quietly for 5 breaths.

Benefits: Promotes quiet time and introspection.

Kids Favorite

33. Padahastasana (Gorilla Pose)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Stand with feet apart.
  2. Bend forward and let arms dangle like a gorilla.
  3. Sway gently from side to side.
  4. Roll up slowly.

Benefits: Relieves tension in the neck and shoulders.

Kids Favorite

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Kneel and curl up into a tiny ball.
  2. Rest your head on the floor.
  3. Keep arms by your sides like a sleeping mouse.
  4. Breathe very softly.

Benefits: Excellent for cooling down before bedtime.

Kids Favorite

35. Candle Pose

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Kneel tall with your back straight.
  2. Bring hands together at your chest like a candle flame.
  3. Take a deep breath in.
  4. Blow the candle out with a long exhale!

Benefits: Teaches deep breathing in a fun, visual way.

Chair Yoga

36. Seated Spinal Twist

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit sideways on a chair, feet flat on the floor.
  2. Inhale deeply and lengthen your spine.
  3. Exhale and gently twist toward the back of the chair.
  4. Hold for 3-5 breaths, return, and switch sides.

Benefits: Improves spinal mobility and digestion safely.

Chair Yoga

37. Seated Mountain (Tadasana)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit at the edge of your chair, feet planted.
  2. Sit up perfectly straight, engage your core.
  3. Roll shoulders back and rest hands on knees.
  4. Breathe deeply for 1 minute.

Benefits: Corrects slouching and strengthens core muscles.

Chair Yoga

38. Neck & Shoulder Rolls

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit comfortably. Roll shoulders up, back, and down 3 times.
  2. Gently drop your right ear toward your right shoulder.
  3. Roll chin to chest, then left ear to left shoulder.

Benefits: Instantly relieves stiffness in the upper body.

Chair Yoga

39. Seated Cat-Cow

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit with hands firmly on your knees.
  2. Inhale, arch your back, and look up (Cow).
  3. Exhale, round your spine, tuck your chin (Cat).
  4. Repeat this smooth motion 5 times.

Benefits: Gently massages the spine and relieves stiffness.

Chair Yoga

40. Seated Side Stretch

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Hold the edge of the chair with your right hand.
  2. Inhale and reach left arm straight up.
  3. Exhale and gently lean body to the right.
  4. Hold 3 breaths, then switch sides.

Benefits: Improves lung capacity and stretches the torso.

Chair Yoga

41. Supported Forward Fold

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit with feet wider than your hips.
  2. Exhale and slowly fold forward from hips.
  3. Let hands slide down legs and head hang loose.
  4. Place hands on knees to push back up slowly.

Benefits: Safe way to decompress the spine and relax.

Chair Yoga

42. Seated Knee-to-Chest

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit up straight. Interlace fingers around right shin.
  2. Gently pull right knee toward your chest.
  3. Keep shoulders relaxed and back straight.
  4. Hold for 3 breaths, release, and switch legs.

Benefits: Stretches the glutes and lower back gently.

Chair Yoga

43. Seated Pigeon Pose

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit tall. Place right ankle over left knee.
  2. Keep right foot flexed to protect the knee.
  3. Lean forward slightly from the hips for a deeper stretch.
  4. Hold 5 breaths and switch legs.

Benefits: Excellent for relieving sciatica and hip pain.

Chair Yoga

44. Seated Eagle Arms

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Cross right arm under left arm at the elbows.
  2. Bend elbows and bring palms to touch (or hold shoulders).
  3. Lift elbows slightly and drop shoulders down.
  4. Breathe into the space between shoulder blades.

Benefits: Deeply stretches the upper back and shoulders.

Chair Yoga

45. Ankle Rotations

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Extend right leg out straight in front of you.
  2. Slowly roll the ankle in a circle 5 times clockwise.
  3. Roll 5 times counter-clockwise.
  4. Point toes, then flex foot. Switch legs.

Benefits: Promotes blood circulation in the lower legs.

Chair Yoga

46. Wrist & Finger Stretches

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Extend arms in front. Make tight fists, then open wide.
  2. Repeat 5 times to warm up fingers.
  3. Point fingers down, use opposite hand to gently press.
  4. Switch hands and repeat.

Benefits: Helps relieve stiffness from arthritis.

Chair Yoga

47. Seated Crescent Moon

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Inhale and reach both arms up overhead.
  2. Clasp hands, keeping index fingers pointing up.
  3. Exhale and lean to the right. Hold 3 breaths.
  4. Return to center and lean left.

Benefits: Opens the chest and improves posture.

Chair Yoga

48. Seated Forward Bend (Single Leg)

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Extend right leg forward, heel on floor, toes pointing up.
  2. Keep left foot flat on floor.
  3. Inhale tall, exhale and hinge forward over right leg.
  4. Hold 3-5 breaths, switch legs.

Benefits: Safely stretches the hamstrings without falling risk.

Chair Yoga

49. Chair Warrior I

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit sideways on chair facing right.
  2. Keep right leg bent on chair. Extend left leg straight back.
  3. Face forward, inhale, raise arms up.
  4. Hold 3-5 breaths, turn around for left side.

Benefits: Builds leg stamina while fully supported.

Chair Yoga

50. Chair Warrior II

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Sit sideways facing right, right knee over ankle.
  2. Extend left leg back, foot flat.
  3. Open chest to the front. Extend arms parallel to floor.
  4. Look over right fingers. Hold 5 breaths, switch sides.

Benefits: Safely opens the hips and chest for older adults.
