Understanding Yoga

A journey of the self, through the self, to the self.

What is Yoga?


Yoga is an ancient practice that originated in India over 5,000 years ago. The word "Yoga" comes from the Sanskrit root "Yuj", meaning to join, yoke, or unite. It represents the beautiful union of the mind, body, and breath.


It is not a religion, but a philosophy of healthy living. Through physical postures (Asanas), breathing techniques (Pranayama), and meditation (Dhyana), yoga promotes harmony within yourself and with nature.
