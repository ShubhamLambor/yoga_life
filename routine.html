3 Min

1. Mindful Warm-up

Start in a seated position. Gently roll your neck from side to side. Do a few shoulder rolls backward and forward. Finish with deep breaths to center yourself.

7 Min

2. Flow & Stretch (Asanas)

Move into Tadasana (Mountain Pose) for grounding. Transition to a gentle forward fold. Come to the mat for Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose) to open your chest, then push back into Balasana (Child's Pose).

5 Min

3. Breath & Relaxation

Sit comfortably and practice Alternate Nostril Breathing (Anulom Vilom) for 3 minutes. Lie on your back in Savasana for the remaining 2 minutes, letting your body completely relax before starting your day.
